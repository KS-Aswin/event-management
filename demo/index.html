About Vela

Vela is a marketplace built for how people actually plan weddings and events in Kerala today — by asking around, comparing prices in WhatsApp groups, and hoping the photographer replies before the venue gets booked out.

We bring venues, photographers, caterers, decorators, DJs, makeup artists, and every other vendor an event needs onto one platform, with transparent pricing, verified reviews, and a simple way to request quotes from several vendors at once.

Vela started with weddings in Kerala and is built to grow with the people who use it — from a birthday for thirty guests to a wedding for a thousand.

What we're not

We're not an event planning company and we don't take a cut of what you pay your vendor unless you ask us to arrange something extra. We're the place you go to find and compare the people who'll actually make your event happen.

Help centre

Answers to the most common questions. Can't find yours? Email us at help@vela.example.

How do I request a quote from a vendor?
Open any vendor's profile and tap "Request quote." Fill in your event date, budget, and a short message — the vendor typically replies within a few hours.
How are vendors verified?
Verified vendors have confirmed their business registration and completed at least one booking through Vela with a reviewed customer. Look for the green "Verified" badge on a profile.
Is it free to use Vela as a customer?
Yes. Searching, comparing vendors, and requesting quotes is free. You pay vendors directly for their services.
How do I list my business on Vela?
Tap "List your business" from the home page, choose "I'm a vendor" when creating your account, and fill in your business profile. Your listing goes live after a quick verification check.
Can I cancel a quote request?
Yes, from your dashboard under "Pending quotations." If a vendor has already responded, we'd appreciate a quick message letting them know.
Privacy

This is a demo product. In a live version, this page would describe exactly what data we collect and why — this summary explains the intent.

We'd collect the information you give us when you create an account or request a quote — your name, contact details, and event information — and share it only with the vendors you choose to contact.

We wouldn't sell your personal data to third parties. Vendors see your enquiry details only after you submit a quote request to them.

Your choices

You'd be able to update or delete your account information at any time from Settings, and control whether you receive email or SMS notifications.
